# The questions every candidate is asked (refined taxonomy, 25 Nov 2026)

Answer every question for every candidate.

1. direction_primary — "Which kind of work do you want next?" Pick ONE specialisation id from ontology-list.txt.
2. direction_secondary — "Any second kind of work you'd also welcome?" ONE specialisation id, or "none".
3. open_to_adjacent — "Show roles in closely related work too?" "yes" or "no".
4. transition — "Are you changing careers?" "no", or "yes" (only if direction_primary is work the résumé does not show).
5. level — "What level of responsibility are you targeting?" one of: "entry" (0–2 yrs / associate), "mid" (experienced individual contributor), "senior" (senior/lead individual contributor or manager of a team), "leader" (head / director or above).
6. years — "How many years of full-time work do you have?" a number (full-time work the résumé actually shows; no education or internships).
7. domain — "Do you want to stay in an industry?" one domain id from the list at the end of ontology-list.txt, or "any".
8. stretch — "Show roles one level above your target too?" "yes" or "no".
9. must_have_qualification — "Do you hold a qualification the résumé doesn't show that some jobs require (e.g. CA, CFA, a licence)?" Always answer "none" unless the résumé itself states one (do not invent).
