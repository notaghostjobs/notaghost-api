Wireless Research Engineer (5G and AI/ML) — Lab45, Wipro · Bangalore, India
The role centres on developing and applying advanced AI and ML techniques to complex problems in 5G wireless communications — designing, implementing and optimising models to improve 5G network performance, reliability and scalability.
Responsibilities: Conduct research on applying AI/ML to 5G wireless communications. Develop and implement AI/ML algorithms for real-world 5G network problems. Work with cross-functional teams to integrate AI/ML solutions into current and future 5G architectures. Analyse complex 5G network data to produce insights that improve performance. Publish research in top-tier venues and present at industry events. Keep up with AI/ML and 5G advancements and apply them within the company. Mentor junior researchers and engineers.
Required skills and experience: PhD or master's degree in electrical engineering, computer science, telecommunications, or a related field. Extensive knowledge of 5G wireless, including network architecture, protocols and standards. Good understanding of 3GPP standards and their framework. Strong expertise in AI/ML, including supervised and unsupervised learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning and statistical modelling. Experience with Python, MATLAB or equivalent tools. Experience developing ML algorithms and state-of-the-art models such as SVM, CNN, LSTM and Transformers. A proven research record through publications in reputable journals and conferences. Strong problem-solving skills; works independently and in teams. Excellent written and verbal communication.
