Director & Head - D2C
Elevation Capital · Bengaluru, Karnataka, India · On-site · Full-time

About the job
This role is with one of our portfolio companies.

About the Role:
This is a high-ownership business leadership role where you will own the P&L and growth agenda end-to-end from category and assortment strategy to customer experience, merchandising, supply chain, marketing levers, profitability and team building.
We are looking for someone who can think like a business owner, identify the biggest growth opportunities, and translate them into execution. You will work closely with the leadership team and cross-functional teams across Product, Tech, Marketing, Operations and Supply Chain to build the next phase of the business.

What You'll Own:
* Own the overall E-commerce P&L, with responsibility for revenue, margins, contribution and sustainable growth
* Identify the biggest growth levers across acquisition, conversion, AOV, repeat purchases, assortment, pricing and customer retention
* Build business plans, revenue forecasts and growth targets; track performance against plan and course-correct quickly
* Balance growth with profitability and build a healthy, scalable business model
* Own the overall category strategy and continuously expand the assortment of products
* Identify whitespace opportunities and new product/category opportunities based on customer needs, trends and business potential
* Drive SKU-level performance identifying winners, fixing underperforming products and improving the overall portfolio mix
* Own the overall customer journey across the app and web store, from discovery to purchase and post-purchase experience
* Improve key ecommerce metrics including traffic, CTR, conversion, AOV, repeat rate and customer lifetime value
* Partner closely with Supply Chain and Operations to build a scalable and efficient operating model
* Build a strong data-driven culture around category and business decisions
* Build and lead a high-performing team across category, merchandising and business operations

Requirements
* 8-12 years of experience in E-commerce, Consumer Internet, D2C or Retail businesses
* Proven experience owning P&L / revenue / contribution for a meaningful business or category
* Strong understanding of E-commerce growth levers conversion, AOV, repeat rate, assortment, pricing, merchandising and customer retention
* Strong analytical orientation with the ability to translate data into business decisions
* Demonstrated ability to build and lead high-performing teams
* Entrepreneurial mindset with a high bias for ownership, speed and execution
* Comfortable operating in an ambiguous, fast-moving environment and building processes as the business scales
