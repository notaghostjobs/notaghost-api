Head of Category – Grocery & FMCG
Zepto · Bengaluru · Full-time

About the role
Zepto is building the fastest grocery delivery in India. We're hiring a Head of Category to own the Grocery & FMCG P&L for the South region — assortment, pricing, margins, supplier terms and in-stock — and to work hand in hand with product and supply chain on the tools that run the category.

What you'll own
- End-to-end P&L for Grocery & FMCG across 40+ dark stores in Bengaluru, Chennai and Hyderabad
- Assortment strategy: what we stock, at what depth, by store cluster
- Pricing and promotions: daily competitive benchmarking, margin management, promo ROI
- Supplier relationships: terms, fill rates, joint business plans with top FMCG brands
- Inventory and in-stock: working with supply chain on forecasting and replenishment
- Building and leading a team of category managers and analysts

What we're looking for
- 8-12 years in category management, buying or merchandising in grocery, FMCG, quick-commerce or e-commerce
- Owned a category P&L of meaningful size; comfortable with margin, pricing and inventory math
- Experience negotiating with national FMCG brands and distributors
- Hands-on with data: SQL or strong analyst partnership; dashboards; pricing benchmarks
- Has built and run a team of 10+; has worked closely with product/engineering on internal tools
- Thrives in speed; Bengaluru-based or willing to relocate

Good to have
- Quick-commerce or dark-store experience
- Experience launching a new city or region
- Engineering background
