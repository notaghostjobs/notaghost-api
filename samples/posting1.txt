Product Analyst — Swiggy Instamart
Bengaluru · Full-time

About the role
We're looking for a Product Analyst to partner with product managers on the Instamart consumer experience. You'll own the metrics for a product area, design and read experiments, and turn data into decisions.

What you'll do
- Own the metrics framework for a consumer product area and drive the weekly business review
- Design experiments with PMs, run power analysis, and write crisp readouts
- Build self-serve dashboards so PMs stop asking you for numbers
- Deep-dive into funnels to find where users drop and why
- Partner with data engineering on event instrumentation

What we need
- 3-6 years as a product/business analyst in a consumer internet company
- Advanced SQL (window functions, CTEs) on large datasets; Python for analysis
- Hands-on experience designing and analysing A/B tests
- Experience with product analytics tools (Mixpanel, Amplitude, or similar)
- Strong written communication — you'll write for leadership

Nice to have
- Experience in quick-commerce, food delivery or e-commerce
- Familiarity with Airflow or dbt
